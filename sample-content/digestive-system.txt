HUMAN DIGESTIVE SYSTEM

Introduction

The digestive system is responsible for breaking down food
into nutrients that the body can absorb and use.

Digestion begins in the mouth. Teeth mechanically break food
into smaller pieces while saliva begins the chemical digestion
of food.

The food then passes through the esophagus and reaches the
stomach. The stomach mixes food with digestive juices and
helps break it down further.

The small intestine is the main site where digestion is
completed and nutrients are absorbed into the bloodstream.

The large intestine absorbs water and electrolytes from the
remaining material and helps form waste.

The digestive system includes several organs including the
mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine,
liver, gallbladder, and pancreas.
